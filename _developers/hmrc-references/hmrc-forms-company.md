**Rates and limits after the 2023 change.** From 1 April 2023 the small profits rate is 19% on
augmented profits up to the lower limit of £50,000 and the main rate is 25% above the upper
limit of £250,000. Between the two the main rate applies with marginal relief. HMRC's formula
(CTM03925) is:

```
Marginal relief = (F x (U - A)) x (N / A)
  F = the standard marginal relief fraction, 3/200 for FY2023 onwards
  U = the upper limit, £250,000
  A = augmented profits
  N = taxable total profits (box 315)
```

Augmented profits are taxable total profits plus exempt ABGH distributions (box 620). Both
limits are divided by (1 + number of associated companies) and time-apportioned for an
accounting period shorter than 12 months. The effective marginal rate between the limits is
26.5%.

Sources: [Marginal Relief for Corporation Tax](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/corporation-tax-marginal-relief),
[CTM03925](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/company-taxation-manual/ctm03925).

## 3. The corporation tax computation

HMRC now prescribes the computation layout. This is new and it is the primary source to build
against: **HMRC Prescription of Computations, version 1.1, published 20 November 2025**,
attached to
[Corporation Tax technical specifications in XBRL and iXBRL format](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/corporation-tax-technical-specifications-xbrl-and-ixbrl)
([PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/691c7cd40dcbf6343e9a2999/ct-computations-format-version-1.1.pdf)).

It covers Section 1 Accounts Adjustments and Section 2 Capital Allowances, with further
sections to follow. HMRC's words: "HMRC are expecting all submissions of computations,
containing the elements set out in those parts, to be in the format set out with the
associated XBRL tags."

Version 1.1's stated change was to **remove the implementation date**. Version 1.0 (1 July
2025) had named accounting periods ending 31 March 2026 onwards. As of September 2026 no
replacement date is published, so treat the format as HMRC's expectation rather than a
hard deadline.

Formatting rules HMRC gives:

- One computation per accounting period, matching the CT600 for that period.
- Each table is a separate HTML table.
- Omit lines with no entry. Include nil lines where a nil is meaningful (for example a pool
  written down to zero).
- Keep tables in the same order with the same descriptors.
- Sterling unless a CFM64000 exemption applies; state the currency in the column header.
- Dates as DD/MM/YYYY.
- Brackets denote a figure running against the direction of its section. In an add-back
  section, a bracketed figure reduces taxable profit.
- Excluded sectors for now: life assurance, oil and gas ring fence, banking, tonnage tax,
  creatives.
- Trading income only, one schedule per trade, parts 1 to 7 in order per trade.

### The classic practitioner layout

The conventional teaching layout, which the CT600 itself follows in boxes 145 to 440, runs:

```
Profit per the accounts (before tax)
  add   disallowable expenditure (depreciation, entertaining, fines, capital items,
        donations, non-trade items)
  less  income taxed elsewhere or not taxable (bank interest, property income,
        dividends, chargeable gains)
  less  capital allowances
  add   balancing charges
= Tax adjusted trading profit                            -> CT600 box 155
  less  trading losses brought forward                   -> box 160
= Net trading profits                                    -> box 165
  add   non-trading loan relationship profits, property income, other income
                                                         -> boxes 170 to 205
  add   net chargeable gains                             -> box 220
= Profits before other deductions and reliefs            -> box 235
  less  deductions and reliefs                           -> box 295
= Profits before qualifying donations and group relief   -> box 300
  less  qualifying charitable donations, group relief    -> boxes 305, 310, 312
= Taxable total profits / profits chargeable to CT       -> box 315
  tax at the applicable rate(s) for each financial year  -> boxes 330 to 430
  less  marginal relief                                  -> box 435
= Corporation Tax chargeable                             -> box 440
```

This ordering is the one HMRC's own form imposes. It is also how ACCA's technical material
sets it out: taxable total profits are "the total of all of a company's income and gains less
qualifying charitable donations", with qualifying charitable donations added back in the
trading adjustment and deducted from total profits.
Secondary source for the wording: ACCA,
[Corporation tax, FTX Foundations in Taxation](https://www.accaglobal.com/us/en/student/exam-support-resources/foundation-level-study-resources/ftx/ftx-technical-articles/corporation-d16.html)
and [Adjustment of profit](https://www.accaglobal.com/gb/en/student/exam-support-resources/fundamentals-exams-study-resources/f6/technical-articles/adjustment-of-profit.html).

### Dividend voucher

There is no statute that prescribes a dividend voucher's content. The legal hooks are indirect:

| Legal hook | What it requires |
| --- | --- |
| Companies Act 2006 s.830 | A company may only make a distribution out of profits available for the purpose |
| Companies Act 2006 s.836 | The distribution must be justified by reference to relevant accounts: last annual, interim, or initial accounts |
| Companies Act 2006 s.386, s.387 | Duty to keep adequate accounting records including day-to-day entries of money received and expended. Breach is an offence by officers in default |
| Companies Act 2006 s.248 | Duty to keep minutes of directors' meetings, retained at least 10 years |
| HMRC SAIM5040 | Dividend tax treatment follows company law on when a dividend is paid. It does not prescribe voucher content |

Conventional content, from practitioner sources rather than statute (secondary):

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Company name and registered number | |

| Shareholder's name and address | |

Do not show a dividend tax credit. That was abolished from 6 April 2016.

Board minute declaring an interim dividend, also practice rather than statute but grounded in

### Minimum tagging

HMRC's position is now full tagging. The accepted-taxonomies page states: "The accounts and
computations must be fully tagged because the taxonomy used contains appropriate XBRL tags."
Historically HMRC published minimum-tagging lists for the older GAAP, UK-IFRS and CT
computational taxonomies. From the CT dimensional taxonomy onward, per the Technical Pack
§1.4, "the taxonomy itself is considered minimum tagging" and the FRC taxonomies "will not
contain a minimum tagging list".

### The CT600 Guide is no longer a PDF

HMRC replaced the box-by-box PDF guide with HTML guidance:
[Completing your Company Tax Return](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-company-tax-return-guide),
first published 30 September 2022, last updated 2 June 2026. The last standalone PDF guides
were CT600 Guide (2008) Version 2 and CT600 Guide (2007) Version 2.

**Negative finding.** HMRC does not publish the box-by-box guide narrative in any
machine-readable form. No JSON, CSV or XML export exists. The closest machine-consumable box
reference is the generated `CT-specDoc` XML in the RIM artefacts zip, which carries the
element-to-box correspondence but not the "how to complete this box" prose.

**Negative finding.** There is no single "box to XBRL tag" master table published as its own
HMRC document. A full mapping has to be assembled from three places: the RIM-artefact spec doc
for CT600 form boxes, the CT Computations Format doc for computation line items, and the
FRC/DPL taxonomy files for the detailed profit and loss figures.

### Known gaps

- HMRC does not publish the CT600 box-by-box guide in any machine-readable form. It is HTML prose only.
- No single "box to XBRL tag" master table is published as its own HMRC document. The mapping must be assembled from the RIM-artefact spec doc (form boxes), the CT Computations Format doc (computation lines) and the FRC/DPL taxonomies (detailed P&L).
- HMRC's accepted-taxonomies page still pins DPL at the FRC 2021 vintage while the FRC suite has moved to 2025 and 2026 with a restructured standalone DPL schema.
- The CT Computations Format v1.1 removed its implementation date. Version 1.0 named accounting periods ending 31 March 2026 onwards. No replacement date is published.
- No commencement order fixing 1 April 2028 for the ECCTA accounts reforms was located. The date rests on the GOV.UK announcement of 9 June 2026 plus professional-body reporting.
- The exact wording of the strengthened audit exemption statement coming in 2028 is not yet published.
- gov.uk's CT600 update history does not record box-level changes between the 2024, 2025 and 2026 refreshes.
